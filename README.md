# Capstone 1: Business Analysis with SQL and Excel

## Project Overview

For my first capstone project, I worked with a fictional company called EmporiUm to analyze sales data using SQL and Excel. The purpose of this project was to gather, organize, and analyze sales information to identify trends, patterns, and opportunities for business growth.

I selected South Carolina as my territory within the South region. Through SQL queries and Excel visualizations, I analyzed online and in-store sales performance and developed data-driven recommendations to help EmporiUm make informed business decisions.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze total online and in-store revenue for South Carolina.
- Identify the first and last transaction dates.
- Examine monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Compare South Carolina's performance with the South region.
- Analyze transaction counts and average transaction amounts by product category.
- Rank states by online revenue .
- Identify opportunities to improve sales performance.

## Tools and Technologies

## SQL Analysis and Key Findings

### 1. South Carolina Total Revenue

**Combined Revenue**

### 2. Monthly and Yearly Revenue Trends

I analyzed monthly revenue from 2022 through 2025 to identify sales trends and fluctuations.

**Online Sales**
- April 2022: $196,115.19
- December 2023: $49,647.77
- January 2024: $54,781.12
- January 2025: $149,045.72

**In-Store Sales**
- July 2022: $13,083.78
- October 2023: $18,688.18
- September 2024: $18,666.48
- August 2025: $34,374.68

These results helped identify changes in monthly sales performance and periods of higher revenue.

### 3. South Carolina Compared to the South Region

I compared South Carolina's revenue with the overall South region, which includes Florida, South Carolina, and Texas.

**South Region Online Revenue:** $7,640,493.08

**South Region In-Store Revenue:** $7,996,850.12

**Combined South Region Revenue:** $15,637,343.20

South Carolina accounted for approximately 8.75% of the South region's combined revenue.

The South region generated slightly more in-store revenue than online revenue.

### 4. Transaction Counts and Average Transaction Amounts by Product Category

I analyzed monthly transaction counts and average transaction amounts for each product category in both online and in-store sales.

Key findings:
- Technology & Accessories showed some of the highest average transaction amounts in both online and in-store sales.
- Online Technology & Accessories had particularly high average transaction amounts during certain months, including January 2025 and April 2022.
- In-store Technology & Accessories also consistently showed strong average transaction amounts across the analyzed periods.
- Transaction counts varied by product category and month.

This analysis helped identify product categories that may be valuable opportunities for targeted marketing and promotions.

### 5. Online Revenue Ranking Across All States

I ranked states based on total online revenue.

- California ranked first with $14,860,811.63.
- South Carolina ranked 18th out of 29 states, with $719,988.75 in online revenue.

This helped identify South Carolina's online performance compared with the other states in the online sales dataset.



## Business Recommendations

Based on my analysis, I recommend that EmporiUm focus on improving sales performance in South Carolina across both online and in-store channels.

Recommended actions include:

- Develop targeted marketing campaigns to attract more customers in South Carolina.
- Explore opportunities to increase online sales and improve in-store performance.
- Consider promotions and advertising for Technology & Accessories, which showed some of the highest average transaction amounts.
- Review monthly sales trends to identify opportunities for promotions during slower sales periods.
- Continue monitoring sales performance to measure the effectiveness of business strategies.

These recommendations could help EmporiUm identify growth opportunities and make more informed business decisions.
