# Capstone 1: Business Analysis with SQL and Excel

## Project Overview
This project analyzes EmporiUm sales data using SQL and Microsoft Excel. The goal is to evaluate sales performance in South Carolina and compare it with the South region to identify business opportunities and make data-driven recommendations.

## Project Territory
- **Territory:** South Carolina
- **Region:** South

## Tools Used
- MySQL Workbench
- SQL
- Microsoft Excel
- GitHub

## Analysis Performed

1. **Total Revenue:** Calculated total online and in-store revenue and identified the first and last transaction dates.
2. **Monthly Revenue Trends:** Analyzed revenue by year and month to identify sales patterns.
3. **Territory vs. Region:** Compared South Carolina revenue with the overall South region.
4. **Product Category Analysis:** Examined monthly transaction counts and average transaction amounts by product category.
5. **Online State Rankings:** Ranked states based on total online revenue.
6. **Business Recommendation:** Developed recommendations based on the sales analysis.

## Key Findings

- South Carolina generated **$719,988.75** in online revenue.
- South Carolina generated **$648,812.56** in in-store revenue.
- Combined South Carolina revenue was **$1,368,801.31**.
- The South region generated **$15,637,343.20** in combined revenue.
- South Carolina ranked **18th among 29 states** in online revenue.
- Technology & Accessories had some of the highest average transaction amounts in both sales channels.

## Business Recommendation

I recommend that EmporiUm focus on increasing both online and in-store sales in South Carolina, with additional attention to growing online revenue. The company should consider targeted promotions for Technology & Accessories and marketing campaigns during slower sales months. These strategies could help improve South Carolina's sales performance and contribution to the South region.
